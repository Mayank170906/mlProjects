📌 Executive Summary

This report presents a comprehensive data science pipeline on the Olist Brazilian E‑Commerce dataset. The objective was to predict whether an order would be delivered on‑time based on order, product, seller, payment, and review features.

After integrating 9 tables and rigorous feature engineering, a LightGBM classifier was trained. The final model achieves a ROC‑AUC of 0.8057 and accuracy of 84%, with excellent recall (0.93) for on‑time deliveries.

The model is exported to ONNX and can be run directly in the browser via ONNX Runtime — enabling low‑latency, client‑side predictions.

📌 Conclusion

This project successfully built a robust LightGBM classifier for predicting on‑time delivery in Brazilian e‑commerce, achieving a ROC‑AUC of 0.8057 and 84% accuracy. The model leverages 11 engineered features, with review_score, payment_value, and approval_time as top drivers.

The ONNX export enables seamless browser deployment via ONNX Runtime, making the model available for real‑time, privacy‑preserving inference. The optimal decision threshold of 0.6656 balances precision and recall, with a strong recall of 0.93 for on‑time deliveries — critical for customer satisfaction monitoring.

Next steps: incorporate seller performance metrics, add geographical distance features, and explore XGBoost / TabNet for further gains.
