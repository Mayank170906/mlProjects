Model Pipeline & Performance

1. Relational Engineering

Unified 9 Olist datasets (~117k records) into 34 predictive features spanning logistical, product, and temporal parameters.

2. LightGBM & Optuna

GBDT binary classifier optimized via Optuna (694 estimators, 127 leaves, depth 10, learning rate 0.062) for ROC-AUC.

3. Precision-Recall Calibration

Exported to ONNX with zipmap=False. Decision threshold calibrated at 0.6656 for optimal F1-score classification.
